Channel jamming mitigation explorer

An interactive companion to BOLT PR #1280, the local resource conservation proposal for mitigating channel jamming.

Parameters

Bucket liquidity split

% of liquidity; protected takes the remainder

Bucket slots

% of max_accepted_htlcs; protected takes the remainder

Per-peer general allocation

k = max(min slots, ⌊general slots × pct⌋)

Channel statistics

Bucket metrics per channel type (max_accepted_htlcs). Liquidity values are % of max_htlc_value_in_flight; saturation is the coupon-collector expectation over the random per-peer slot assignment.

Channel distribution

Channel percentiles

General-bucket routability

Routability by payment size and hop count. Darker means more of the payment flow stays in the general bucket. The outlined column is the selected payment size — drag across the squares to move it.

Table view
  • Per-hop numbers overstate routability. The per-route curve is the decision-relevant view, and it composes hops as independent events — real routes correlate, since a payment picks well-funded channels, so treat the route curve as a lower bound rather than an estimate.
  • The base value per edge is its advertised max_htlc, the observable lower bound on max_htlc_value_in_flight_msat. Gossip carries neither that field nor max_accepted_htlcs, so the slot counts are assumptions, not measurements, and every number here moves with them.
  • "% of edges" is not "% of payments" is not "% of liquidity". The weighting toggle exists so the divergence is visible rather than hidden behind one framing.
