Channel jamming mitigation explorer

An interactive companion to BOLT PR #1280, the local resource conservation proposal for mitigating channel jamming.

Parameters

Bucket liquidity split

% of liquidity; protected takes the remainder

Bucket slots

% of max_accepted_htlcs; protected takes the remainder

Per-peer general allocation

k = max(min slots, ⌊general slots × pct⌋)

Channel statistics

Bucket metrics per channel type (max_accepted_htlcs). Liquidity values are % of max_htlc_value_in_flight; saturation is the coupon-collector expectation over the random per-peer slot assignment.

Channel distribution

Channel percentiles
