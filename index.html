Channel statistics

This section applies the parameters selected above to theoretical channels with different max_accepted_htlcs.

Filtering

When enabled, edges that advertise a max_htlc less than the selected amount do not enforce per-peer general liquidity restrictions. A payment through them may use the whole general bucket instead. Turning Unlimited Below Threshold off removes this exemption and pins the threshold to zero.

General routability
  • Sort nodes by capacity: the total max_htlc advertised across all of a node's edges.
  • Partition nodes by capacity percentiles, and randomly sample sources and destinations in each.
  • Find the shortest path for each route, and determine whether the payment amount can be facilitated in the general bucket.
