Channel jamming mitigation explorer

An interactive companion to BOLT PR #1280, the local resource conservation proposal for mitigating channel jamming.

Parameters

Bucket liquidity split

% of liquidity; protected takes the remainder

Bucket slots

fixed counts, not scaled by channel size; protected takes the remainder

Per-peer general allocation

k = max(min slots, ⌊general slots × pct⌋)

Filtering

Edges that advertise a max_htlc less than the selected amount are excluded from further analysis. This filtering is relevant because some channels are simply too small to forward payments with any additional restrictions placed on them.

Advertised max_htlc across the graph's directed edges, four bars per power of ten. Greyed bars are filtered out; the marked bar is cut at the threshold, since a bar spans a range of values.

What this does not account for

  • Unknown max_htlc_value_in_flight. This value is not gossiped. We use max_htlc_msat as a proxy, and where a direction advertises none we impute 99% of capacity.
  • One HTLC in flight. We assume a single HTLC occupying the outgoing channel's allocation on its incoming channel, and do not account for liquidity distribution.
